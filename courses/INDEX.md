Platform notes:
- Coloso lessons are studio-recorded, scripted-then-spoken, polite register, usually one instructor,
  screen capture + face cam. Chapter intros often restate lesson goals; outros summarize. Timing is
  usually generous.
- Class101 lessons are similar but more conversational; the instructor frequently addresses the
  student directly ("여러분").
- Independent Chinese courses (Krenz, Evan Lee, Tenten, PANDA) are often recorded live or as long
  screen-share sessions with student Q&A, off-mic remarks, and dense filler. Expect more ASR noise,
  self-corrections, and references to student homework.
