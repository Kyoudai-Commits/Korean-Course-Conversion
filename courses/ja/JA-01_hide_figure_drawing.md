# Master Figure Drawing: Capturing Natural Anatomy from Every Angle (Hide) — JA-01
- Language: Japanese, polite. Coloso studio format.
- Instructor: Hide (ヒデ). TTS: "HEE-day", not English "hide".
- Discipline: figure drawing, gesture, anatomy for illustrators; drawing the body from
  multiple angles (煽り low angle / 俯瞰 high angle), foreshortening, natural poses.
- Software (verify): Clip Studio Paint likely; may use 3Dデッサン人形 for reference.
- Core concepts: 重心 (balance), コントラポスト (contrapposto), 動き/流れ (line of action / flow),
  骨格 (skeleton) → 筋肉 (muscle) → 肉付け (fleshing out), 箱/円柱で捉える (blocking with boxes and
  cylinders), 関節 (joints), 可動域 (range of motion), 正中線 (center line), 肩幅/腰幅 (shoulder/hip width),
  頭身 (head count: 8頭身 = 8 heads tall), 煽り/俯瞰 (worm's-eye / bird's-eye), パースのかかった体
  (foreshortened body), 縮む/伸びる (compress / stretch).
- Anatomy names likely: 鎖骨 clavicle, 肩甲骨 shoulder blade, 胸郭 rib cage, 骨盤 pelvis, 大胸筋 pecs,
  三角筋 deltoid, 僧帽筋 trapezius, 広背筋 lats, 腹直筋 abs, 前鋸筋 serratus, 大腿四頭筋 quads,
  ハムストリング hamstrings, 腓腹筋 calves, 上腕二頭筋 biceps, 前腕 forearm, 膝 knee, 肘 elbow, 手首 wrist.
  Prefer the English an art student uses (pecs, lats, delts) when the instructor is casual; formal names
  when he is being precise.
- Traps: 影 vs 陰; 面 (plane of the body); アタリ; 腰 can mean waist, hips, or lower back — pick by context;
  足 = leg or foot (context); 手 = hand or arm; 首 = neck (not head); 顔の向き = head direction.
- Voice: methodical, encouraging. Preserve "let's" walk-throughs.
- Verify: software, whether he demonstrates on real photos, chapter list.
