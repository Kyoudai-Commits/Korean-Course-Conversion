# Illustration Improvement Program (Naoki Saito) — JA-04
- Language: Japanese, polite but energetic YouTuber cadence; rhetorical questions, punchy summaries.
- Instructor: Naoki Saito (さいとうなおき). TTS: "NAH-oh-kee SAH-ee-toh". Well-known illustrator
  (Pokémon TCG, Duel Masters) and YouTube educator. Refer to him as "Saito" or "Naoki Saito".
- Discipline: illustration fundamentals and critique. Heavy on 添削 (paint-over critiques of student
  work), mindset, "what separates people who improve (伸びる人) from those who don't".
- Software (verify): Photoshop primarily; may reference Clip Studio Paint.
- Core concepts and his recurring vocabulary:
  - 目線誘導 leading the eye · 情報量のコントロール controlling the amount of detail ·
    メリハリ contrast/variation · 密度 density · 主役/脇役 main subject / supporting elements ·
    見せたい部分 the part you want to show · 一枚絵 a standalone illustration ·
    キャラの魅力 character appeal · 表情 expression · 立体感 volume · 空気感 atmosphere ·
    画面 the picture / the composition ("画面が寂しい" = the image feels empty) ·
    惜しい "so close" · 違和感 "something feels off" · 上手く見える looks skillful ·
    画力 skill · 伸びる improve rapidly · 上達 improvement · 練習法 practice method ·
    模写 master copy / copying · 観察 observation · 引き算 subtracting · 足し算 adding ·
    描き込み rendering detail · ラフ rough · 完成度 level of finish · クオリティ quality ·
    色数 number of colors · 彩度を落とす lower saturation · 明暗の差 value contrast ·
    トーン (value tone) · グレースケールで確認 check in grayscale · 逆光 backlight · リムライト rim light ·
    フォーカス focus · ピント (photographic focus) · 手前/奥 foreground / background.
- Structure (verify): likely lectures + before/after critiques. In critiques he speaks about a third
  party's work ("この方の絵") — translate as "this artist's piece / this student's piece".
- Traps: 惜しい is not "regrettable" — it's "almost there"; 絵 (picture) vs え (uh); 目 vs め (filler);
  伸びる (improve) vs 延びる; 映える (looks good / pops) vs 生える; はい vs 灰; 白 vs 城 (both しろ);
  線 vs 千. He uses ぶっちゃけ (honestly), ぶっちゃけ言うと → "to be blunt".
- Voice: keep his energy, direct address, and takeaway lines ("結論から言うと" = "Bottom line:").
  Do not flatten his rhetorical build-ups; do trim repeated はい.
