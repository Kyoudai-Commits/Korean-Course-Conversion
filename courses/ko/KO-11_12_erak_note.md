# Realistic Character Art (KO-11) / Step-by-Step Face Coloring from Basic to Advanced (KO-12) — Erak Note
- Language: Korean, polite. Coloso.
- Instructor: Erak Note (에라크노트). TTS: "EH-rahk NOTE". Keep as two words.
- Discipline: realistic digital painting, especially faces and skin. KO-12 is a focused face-rendering
  course (밑색 → 명암 → 피부 → 이목구비 → 머리카락 → 보정). KO-11 is broader realistic character work.
- Software: Photoshop (verify brush set).
- Core concepts: 피부 표현 skin rendering, 피부 톤, 붉은 기 (red tones in skin: nose/cheeks/knuckles),
  혈색 (complexion / blood tone), 피하 지방 (subcutaneous fat), 골격 (bone structure under the face),
  광대 cheekbone, 턱선 jawline, 미간 glabella/between the brows, 인중 philtrum, 콧방울 nostrils/ala,
  콧대 nose bridge, 눈두덩 eyelid/orbital area, 눈꺼풀 eyelid, 애교살 aegyo-sal (under-eye fat pad — keep
  romanized or "under-eye bag/roll" with note), 입술 lips, 입꼬리 corners of the mouth, 눈꼬리 outer
  corner of the eye, 이마 forehead, 관자놀이 temple, 머리카락 결/덩어리 hair flow/clumps, 피부 질감 skin
  texture, 모공 pores, 솜털 peach fuzz, SSS / 피부 투과 subsurface scattering, 반사광, 주광 key light,
  보조광 fill light, 림라이트, 명암 경계, 중간톤, 색온도 color temperature, 난색/한색 warm/cool,
  채도 조절, 보정 (final adjustments), 사진 자료 photo reference, 눈동자 iris/pupil, 흰자 sclera,
  속눈썹 lashes, 눈썹 brows, 치아 teeth.
- Traps: 광대 (cheekbone) vs 광대 (clown); 인중 vs 인증; 콧대 vs 코대; 눈두덩 vs 눈두덕; 결 vs 겹;
  붉은 기 = redness, 기 not "energy"; 혈색 = complexion.
- Voice: patient, granular. Preserve step numbering when he counts steps.
- Verify: brush names (custom brushes often referred to by nickname).
