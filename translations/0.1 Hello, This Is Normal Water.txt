Hello.
I'm Sangsoo Jeong, and I work as a concept artist and illustrator.
How did I end up becoming an illustrator and meeting all of you, my classmates?
Ever since I was little, I've loved creative activities.
By chance, my kindergarten turned out to be one attached to an art academy.
I have fond memories of making toys out of colored paper and milk cartons every day and drawing pictures with crayons.
During my school years, I found studying genuinely annoying and boring, so I'd just go to school thinking only about getting home to play games.
Even so, art class was the one thing I really enjoyed.
I think I was obsessed with making things and owning them.
For several years I stayed shut in at home, totally absorbed in paper crafting.
I even ran a paper model café.
As time passed and I entered my twenties, I began to worry about my future.
What on earth am I going to do for a living?
And I agonized over what I was good at and what I actually liked.
Then I happened to come across some digital art on the internet.
And naturally I started to get interested in digital drawing.
As long as I had a tablet and a PC, I wouldn't need separate materials or a studio, I could easily connect with people over the internet, and it could satisfy both my urge to create and my urge to own things. That was what made it so appealing to me.
Looking back now, I think I chose my profession really well.
Through the pictures I draw, I can give people a small sense of being moved.
I can communicate with lots of people, and it makes me feel like I'm really alive.
And it brings in money too.
How did the rest of you get started with drawing?
There are probably all sorts of reasons, but one thing's certain: you're taking this course in order to draw better, and most of you will keep drawing in the future and become colleagues of mine, working in the same industry.
Through this class, I hope that you, as both my classmates and my fellow creators, will be inspired by and influence one another, grow together, and become artists who make each other shine and move people.
That's what I truly hope.
In this class, I want to focus on raising your value.
I'll lay the groundwork so that you can confidently stand out, even in a market that's already a red ocean.
I hope this becomes the kind of class you recall every time you draw, and one that stays memorable long after.
I'd love it if you made great strides and grew through this course.
I look forward to working with you, everyone.
